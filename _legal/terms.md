## What Onyx is

Onyx is a personal health, fitness, and nutrition app for iPhone and Apple Watch. It reads metrics from Apple Health, computes a daily recovery score, generates training and nutrition guidance, reads imported bloodwork, and offers an AI coach that reasons against your current state. It is not a medical device, not a diagnostic tool, and not a replacement for a clinician.

## Your account

There is no Onyx account. Your data lives on your iPhone. If you're signed in to iCloud, Onyx syncs your settings and preferences to your own private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit; we can't read it, and we don't hold a copy. Your health data is not stored in iCloud. Apple's In-App Purchase system handles payment; we never see your payment details.

## Apple Health permissions

Onyx asks permission to read and write specific Apple Health metrics, in two stages: a core set at setup and a smaller second request the first time you open the Body tab. You grant or revoke each one in iOS Settings. Onyx can't function as designed without read access, but you decide what's shared, and the app degrades honestly rather than inventing numbers it can't measure.

## Coach and other AI features

Coach, food photo parsing, menu reading, bloodwork extraction, and the written narratives are powered by an AI model and may produce inaccurate or incomplete results. Treat them as a thoughtful starting point, not a final answer. Onyx asks your permission before it sends anything to its AI provider, and you can turn AI features off at any time.

Bloodwork extraction in particular reads numbers off an image. Check the values against your original lab report before acting on anything. Onyx shows you the extracted panel for review before it saves, and that review step exists for a reason.
